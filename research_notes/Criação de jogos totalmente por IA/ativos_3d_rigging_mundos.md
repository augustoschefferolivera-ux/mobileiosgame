## Key Question 1 — Text/image-to-3D generators: which produce the cleanest game-ready topology and PBR textures, and which offer retopology, quad meshes, part segmentation?

### Takeaway
As of Oct 2026 the commercial leaders for game-ready output are **Tripo 3.x (Tripo Studio)** and **Meshy 7/7.1**, both offering PBR textures, polycount/topology controls, auto-rigging and FBX/GLB/USD export under paid commercial licences (~US$20/mo entry tier). Among open-weight models, **Microsoft TRELLIS.2 (MIT, Dec 2025, PBR incl. opacity)** and **Tencent Hunyuan3D 2.1 (PBR, training code, but a Tencent Community License that excludes the EU/UK/South Korea)** are the strongest; none of the open models produce clean quad topology natively — all emit dense triangle meshes that need retopology/decimation before mobile use. Native **part segmentation** exists in Hunyuan3D-Part (P3-SAM/X-Part, Sept 2025) and PartCrafter (NeurIPS 2025, MIT); **quad/retopo** is offered as a service feature by Tripo, Meshy ("Smart Topology"), Hitem3D ("automatic retopology"), and in SPAR3D's quad-remesh option, but independent quality tests are scarce.

### Inferences
- No current generator (commercial or open) emits "artist-grade" quad topology by default; the realistic 2026 pipeline is **generate → (service retopo/low-poly mode or Quad Remesher/Instant Meshes) → decimate/LOD → bake PBR → export GLB/FBX/USDZ**. Tripo's "smart low-poly", Meshy's "Smart Topology" and Hitem3D's "automatic retopology" exist precisely because raw outputs are dense (TRELLIS.2's default GLB export targets 1M faces; Sparc3D 2.0 defaults to 1536³).
- For an iOS studio in Brazil, Tencent's Community License is usable (Brazil is inside the Territory) but **shipping outputs to EU/UK/South Korea players is contractually prohibited** ("must not use … Output … outside the Territory") — a serious blocker for a globally distributed App Store game unless a separate licence is negotiated. TRELLIS.2 (MIT), TripoSG/PartCrafter (MIT) and SPAR3D (free <US$1M revenue) avoid this.
- Commercial SaaS tiers (~US$20/mo Meshy Pro / Tripo Pro) are the cheapest route to commercial-use rights with rigging + PBR included; free tiers are non-commercial (Tripo) or attribution-required CC BY 4.0 (Meshy).
- Reviews consistently rank Tripo above Meshy for geometry/rigging and Meshy above Tripo for texturing, but there is no independent, reproducible game-engine benchmark; the Sloyd arena (vote-based) and Meshy's own benchmark are each vendor-run.

## Key Question 2 — Rigging and animation: auto-rigging (Meshy/Tripo, Anything World, Mixamo status, UniRig, Magic Articulate), text/video-to-motion (DeepMotion, Kinetix, Cascadeur, Motorica, Rokoko Vision, Move.ai, Plask, MotionGPT-style), facial animation (Audio2Face/ACE)

### Takeaway
Auto-rigging has moved from Mixamo (free but effectively unmaintained, biped-only, with recurring auth outages in 2025–26) to (a) built-in rigging in Tripo Studio / Meshy and (b) open research models from Tripo/VAST (**UniRig**, SIGGRAPH 2025, MIT; successor **SkinTokens/TokenRig**, 2026, MIT) and ByteDance Seed (**MagicArticulate**, CVPR 2025; **Puppeteer**, NeurIPS 2025, Apache-2.0, FBX export), which handle quadrupeds/creatures. Video-to-motion consolidated in 2026 (Rokoko Vision 3.0 merged into Rokoko Create, July 2026); text-to-motion remains mostly research-grade (MotionGPT3, Oct 2025 weights) or bundled in SaaS (Rokoko Create, DeepMotion SayMotion, Cinevva). For facial animation, **NVIDIA Audio2Face-3D went open source on 24–26 Sept 2025** (SDK/plugins MIT, training framework Apache) with UE5 and Maya plugins but **no Unity plugin**.

## Key Question 5 — Mobile constraints: getting mobile-friendly meshes automatically (decimation, LODs, texture atlasing); USDZ/RealityKit/SceneKit/Metal considerations for iOS

### Takeaway
Apple's only published numeric guidance remains the WWDC 2018 AR Quick Look rule of thumb — **≤100K polygons, one 2048×2048 texture set, ≤10 s animation** — reaffirmed by Apple engineers in 2021 (RealityKit apps have "more wiggle room"; generate multiple model versions per device) and explicitly *not* updated with new numbers in 2024 ("iterate: start low-poly/low-res, add detail while performance headroom remains"). Since AI generators emit 100K–1M+ triangle meshes, automatic reduction is mandatory: the open-source toolchain **meshoptimizer/gltfpack (`-si` simplify, `-tc` KTX2, quantization, `-c/-cc` compression) + glTF-Transform (`simplify`, `weld`, `resize`, `webp/ktx2`, `optimize`)** can be scripted into a pipeline; generator-side options exist (TRELLIS.2 `decimation_target`, TripoSG `--faces`, Tripo "smart low-poly", Meshy "Smart Topology", SPAR3D quad remesh).

### Cited Findings
- Apple engineer (Nov 2021): WWDC 2018 Session 603 recommended **up to 100K polygons, one set of 2048×2048 textures, up to 10 s animation**; "still what we recommend" for AR Quick Look so **all devices down to iPhone 7** display well; **RealityKit apps have "a bit more wiggle room"** and should "generate multiple versions of your model and use different model depending on the power of the target device"; whether 100K is per-model or per-scene, and file-size limits, were not addressed — [Apple Developer Forums thread 693867](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/693867)
- Apple DTS (May 2024, visionOS/RealityKit): declined to give numeric triangle/texture budgets; recommends an iterative cycle (start low-poly + low-res textures → check quality → add detail only if performance headroom); points to WWDC24 session "Optimize your 3D assets for spatial computing" (10186) for recommended maximum triangle counts (numbers in an image, not retrievable here) — [Apple Developer Forums thread 751764](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/751764)
- A June 2024 developer question on **LOD in Reality Composer Pro / USDZ** (three meshes high/med/low by distance) received **no answer** — built-in USDZ LOD support unconfirmed — [Apple Developer Forums thread 757297](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/757297); one developer anecdotally reports LOD meshes/textures stream from disk on demand (unverified) — [Apple forum search result](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/693867)
- USDZ background and tooling (Reality Converter converts OBJ/FBX/glTF/USD → USDZ; UsdPreviewSurface baseline) — Apple's documentation page body could not be rendered in this sandbox; see [Apple "Creating USD files for Apple devices"](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/realitykit/creating-usd-files-for-apple-devices) and a third-party overview — [Netguru AR Quick Look & USDZ](https://netguru.com/blog/ar-quick-look-and-usdz)
- **meshoptimizer** (MIT; v1.3): vertex-cache/overdraw optimisation, **`meshopt_simplify`** (attribute-aware, permissive variants), LOD-chain building with screen-space-error selection, vertex quantization, mesh compression codecs, meshlets; discusses mobile GPUs (PowerVR/Apple tiled rendering); **gltfpack** CLI; 8.5k stars — [GitHub zeux/meshoptimizer](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer)
- **gltfpack** options: **`-si R`** simplify to ratio R (0–1); **`-tc`** KTX2/BasisU textures (`KHR_texture_basisu`), **`-tw`** WebP; **`-c` / `-cc` / `-cz`** meshopt compression (loaders need decoder; three.js r122+, Babylon 5.0+); **`-mi`** GPU instancing; **`-kn/-km/-ke`** keep names/materials/extras; `KHR_mesh_quantization` on by default (`-noq` to disable), `-vpf` float positions; Draco not supported; vendor extensions dropped; Unity/Unreal extension support not documented (check engine docs) — [gltfpack README](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer/blob/master/gltf/README.md)
- **glTF-Transform** (MIT; ~2.0k stars): CLI `optimize`, `draco`, `meshopt`, `resize`, `webp`, `ktx2 (uastc/etc1s)`, `dedup`, `prune`, `resample` — [GitHub donmccurdy/glTF-Transform](https://github.com/donmccurdy/glTF-Transform); `simplify()` wraps meshoptimizer: welds first ("split vertices will limit the simplifier"), `ratio` (default 0 = max simplification bounded by `error`), `error` (default 0.0001 of mesh radius), `lockBorder` for seamless terrain chunks; converts indices to 16-bit when ≤65,534 vertices — [simplify.ts](https://github.com/donmccurdy/glTF-Transform/blob/main/packages/functions/src/simplify.ts)
- Generator-side reduction controls: TRELLIS.2 GLB export `decimation_target` (default 1,000,000) + remeshing — [GitHub microsoft/TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2); TripoSG `--faces` cap — [GitHub TripoSG](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSG); SPAR3D `remesh: none/triangle/quad` and texture-resolution option — [GitHub SPAR3D](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-point-aware-3d); Tripo "smart low-poly optimization for XR and gaming" — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250820723593/en/Tripo-the-Frontrunner-of-3D-AI-Boom-Supercharges-New-Era-in-Content-Creation-with-3.0-Upgrade); Meshy "Smart Topology" low-poly model (Jul 2026) — [docs.meshy.ai changelog](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/changelog)
- Retopology tools: **Instant Meshes** (BSD-3 licence, 2015 SIGGRAPH Asia "Instant Field-Aligned Meshes"; Modo uses the algorithm since 10.2; prebuilt Win/Mac/Linux binaries; 6.2k stars, 93 open issues) — [GitHub wjakob/instant-meshes](https://github.com/wjakob/instant-meshes), [LICENSE.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wjakob/instant-meshes/master/LICENSE.txt); **Quad Remesher** reported at **US$79 perpetual per user** with free trial (SEO source, low reliability) — [SuperRendersFarm](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/quad-remesher-blender-retopology); **QuadMend** Blender add-on wraps Instant Meshes + QuadriFlow and keeps the better result; vendor test: 22 usable results vs Instant Meshes 4, QuadriFlow 2 — [SuperHive Market](https://superhivemarket.com/products/quadmend); **InstaLOD Studio** (Jul 2026 press release) updated Quad Remesh for dense/messy meshes → all-quad topology — [Abstract3D](https://abstract3d.com/?p=3573)
- Splat formats for mobile delivery: SPZ ~10× smaller than PLY — [GitHub nianticlabs/spz](https://github.com/nianticlabs/spz); MetalSplatter native iOS renderer — [GitHub scier/MetalSplatter](https://github.com/scier/MetalSplatter)

### Inferences
- A defensible automated iOS budget derived from Apple's statements: **hero character ≤ 30–50K tris, props ≤ 5–10K, whole frame ≤ ~100K tris on the oldest supported device**, textures ≤2048² (one atlas per asset), 2–3 LODs generated by `meshopt_simplify`/`gltf-transform simplify` at ratios ~0.5/0.25/0.1 with `error` bounds — then profile on device as Apple insists. (The per-asset split is my inference; Apple gives only the 100K/2048² figure.)
- Because glTF/GLB is the lingua franca of every generator while iOS prefers USDZ, the pipeline should be **GLB → optimize (gltfpack/glTF-Transform) → Reality Converter/usdzconvert → USDZ** for RealityKit, or GLB/FBX import for Unity/Unreal iOS builds; KTX2/BasisU textures are useful for web/Unity but USDZ expects PNG/JPEG, so texture compression for RealityKit happens at Apple's import (ASTC) rather than in the file.

## Key Question 6 — Blender automation: Blender MCP, AI scripting of Blender (Python via agents), add-ons for cleanup/retopo (Quad Remesher, Instant Meshes), export pipelines

### Takeaway
**Blender MCP (ahujasid/mcp-for-blender, MIT, 30.4k★)** is the de-facto standard bridge: an LLM writes and executes `bpy` Python through `execute_blender_code`, with visual verification (multi-angle/wireframe renders) and built-in asset/generator hooks (Poly Haven, Sketchfab, Poly Pizza, **Tripo, Hyper3D Rodin, Hunyuan3D**). Tripo ships its own **tripo-mcp** (MIT, alpha) relying on the Tripo Blender add-on. Retopo/cleanup remains classic tooling — Quad Remesher (paid), Instant Meshes/QuadriFlow (free, wrapped by QuadMend) — that an agent can drive via Python; several research rigs (Puppeteer FBX export, MaterialAnything, UniRig VRM) themselves depend on specific `bpy`/Blender versions, which matters for pipeline pinning.

### Cited Findings
- **MCP for Blender** (formerly blender-mcp; "Community plugin to control Blender 3D with any LLM"; **MIT; 30.4k stars, 2.8k forks, 244 commits**): socket server two-way comms; create/modify/delete objects, materials; **visual verification** (multi-angle, camera, wireframe, X-ray, render views); `execute_blender_code` runs arbitrary Python; asset sources **Poly Haven (no key), Sketchfab (key), Poly Pizza (free key)**; AI model generation via **Tripo, Hyper3D Rodin, Hunyuan3D** (Tencent Cloud mainland/international toggle); a "Premium" option generates without your own API keys; requires **Blender ≥3.0, Python ≥3.10**; installs via one-line script, `uvx mcp-for-blender`, Docker or pipx; **no auth/encryption on the socket (keep on localhost)**; optional safe mode blocks file/network/process access; long requests may time out; Poly Haven downloads block the main thread; anonymous usage telemetry on by default (content telemetry opt-in) — [GitHub ahujasid/blender-mcp](https://github.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp)
- Practitioner assessments: good for scene assembly/materials/lighting/repetitive edits, **does not generate meshes itself** — [3D AI Studio (Aug 2026)](https://www.3daistudio.com/3d-generator-ai-comparison-alternatives-guide/blender-mcp); results are correct primitive compositions with proper naming/materials/scale "ready for you to refine" (Apr 2026) — [StraySpark](https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/blender-mcp-natural-language-3d-modeling); overview — [yuv.ai](https://yuv.ai/blog/blender-mcp); listed in AI-native landscape — [jimmysong.io](https://jimmysong.io/en/ai/blender-mcp)
- **tripo-mcp** ("Official MCP server for Tripo", alpha, MIT, 211 stars): generates assets from prompts via Tripo's API and imports into Blender; requires Python 3.10+, Blender, **Tripo AI Blender Addon** with its MCP server running, Claude Desktop or Cursor — [GitHub VAST-AI-Research/tripo-mcp](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/tripo-mcp)
- Blender-version pinning seen in research tooling: Puppeteer FBX export requires `bpy==4.2.0` — [GitHub Seed3D/Puppeteer](https://github.com/Seed3D/Puppeteer); Material Anything's Blender scripts target 3.2.2 (newer versions may break node names) — [GitHub 3DTopia/MaterialAnything](https://github.com/3DTopia/MaterialAnything); UniRig uses a modified VRM-Addon-for-Blender only for .vrm I/O — [GitHub UniRig](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/UniRig); MagicArticulate provides a Blender script to read rigs from GLB — [GitHub MagicArticulate](https://github.com/Seed3D/MagicArticulate); MotionGPT3 renders via Blender — [GitHub MotionGPT3](https://github.com/OpenMotionLab/MotionGPT3)
- Retopo add-ons: Quad Remesher (US$79 perpetual, Blender 3.0+ claimed; tested up to 4.1 in the cited article — dated) — [SuperRendersFarm (low reliability)](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/quad-remesher-blender-retopology); Instant Meshes (free, BSD) — [GitHub wjakob/instant-meshes](https://github.com/wjakob/instant-meshes); QuadMend (wraps Instant Meshes + QuadriFlow) — [SuperHive Market](https://superhivemarket.com/products/quadmend)
- Engine-side MCP servers that complete the agent pipeline after Blender export: **unity-mcp** (MIT, 14.8k★, Unity 2021.3–6.x) — [GitHub CoplayDev/unity-mcp](https://github.com/CoplayDev/unity-mcp); **unreal-mcp** (MIT, experimental, UE 5.5+) — [GitHub chongdashu/unreal-mcp](https://github.com/chongdashu/unreal-mcp)
- Headless optimisation CLIs usable from agent scripts: gltfpack and glTF-Transform (see Q5) — [gltfpack README](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer/blob/master/gltf/README.md); [GitHub glTF-Transform](https://github.com/donmccurdy/glTF-Transform)

### Inferences
- A fully agentic Blender pipeline in 2026 looks like: agent → Blender MCP (`generate_3d` via Tripo/Rodin/Hunyuan3D or import GLB) → `bpy` cleanup (merge by distance, QuadriFlow remesh or Quad Remesher operator, decimate modifier for LODs, UV pack, bake) → export GLB/FBX → gltfpack/glTF-Transform → unity-mcp/unreal-mcp import or Reality Converter → USDZ. All pieces exist and are MIT-licensed except Quad Remesher and the SaaS generators.
- Security: because `execute_blender_code` runs arbitrary Python over an unauthenticated local socket, agent-driven Blender should run in an isolated environment/VM for any pipeline that processes untrusted prompts or assets.
